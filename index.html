02 Structure

The operator runs the plant. BB3 Earth runs the finance.

Three parties, each with a clear role. Our operating partner runs the machines and the sites. We structure the finance, issue the tokens and look after investors.

  1. Plant and operations

    Physical layer

    The operating partner

    Runs an award-winning machine that converts organic, industrial and municipal waste into green commodities, biofuel and energy. Runs every facility on the ground.

    Holds
    The land, the plant3, and the intake and offtake contracts

  2. Tokenised debt

    Financial and legal layer

    BB3 Earth

    Sets up each facility as a ring-fenced SPV and issues tokenised debt against it on a regulated platform. Runs investor relations, governance and distributions for the life of the instrument.

    Responsible for
    SPV structure, token issuance, the investor register and distributions

  3. Return

    The investor

    Will receive coupons paid from facility revenue and published on-chain, so each payment can be checked. Will also receive green certificates tied to the output of the facility they funded.

    Receives
    The debt instrument, coupons and incentivised green returns

From Phase 2, 2027

Machine data goes on-chain. Each green certificate can then be traced to a reading from the machine itself, not to a figure reported after the fact.

04 Green credentials

The green claim rests on what the plant produces.

Every layer of the instrument links to a real environmental outcome. It is not a label added afterwards, and not an offset bought elsewhere.

Bio-product manufacturing

In place

The facilities produce certified bio-products. Returns are tied to real, measurable output, not to a financial derivative of it.

SPV transparency

Phase 1

Each facility runs in its own Special Purpose Vehicle. Investors see the assets held, the debt outstanding and the revenue used to pay coupons.

Green certificates

With the instrument

Investors will receive green certificates alongside their debt instrument, tied to the production output of the facility they funded.

On-chain verification

Phase 2, 2027

Phase 1 reports the debt instrument on-chain. Phase 2 adds real-time plant data, so output can be checked at the source.

BB3 Earth cannot verify plant output in Phase 1.

06 Roadmap

Four milestones between now and 2028.

The market for real-world asset tokenisation is growing fast. Green infrastructure debt sits where two trends meet: ESG capital mandates and digital asset adoption.

  1. Q1 2027

    Phase 1 · next

    Debt goes on-chain

    The South Carolina SPV debt is tokenised on a regulated platform. Fractional, transferable instruments are issued to investors for the first time.

  2. 2027

    Phase 2

    Machine data on-chain

    Real-time plant inputs and outputs are recorded on-chain. Every green certificate is then backed by a data point from the machine.

  3. 31 Dec 2027

    Raise 2

    Netherlands raise closes

    The ~€50m raise closes with the South Carolina SPV live on-chain as its reference asset. Dutch investors enter a vehicle that is already funded and paying.

  4. 2028

    Phase 3

    The BB3 token and tokenised green funds

    BB3 Earth plans its own platform token and tokenised green funds, connecting the platform, its investors and the projects it finances.

Why now

ESG capital mandates

Institutional investors are under pressure to hold green assets they can verify. BB3 Earth offers infrastructure debt with on-chain reporting, built for that mandate.

Real-world asset tokenisation

Tokenisation opens assets that were too illiquid, too opaque or too small to reach. The market is growing quickly, and green infrastructure is one of its most credible uses.
